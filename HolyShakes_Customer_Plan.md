## 3. Your Gmail drafts (4 templates, no recipient set)
1. **Office treat day? Free HolyShakes tasting for your team** (offices)
2. **A shake bar for your clients' events** (planners and venues)
3. **Fundraiser idea for [Team/School name]** (sports and schools)
4. **Local partnership idea: HolyShakes x [Business name]** (gyms, cafés, businesses)

Before sending: fill in every `[bracket]`, add your phone number and the correct street address (sources disagree between 8329 and 8239 120 St), and only email addresses a business publishes itself. Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) requires your name, a way to reach you, and an easy opt-out, which are already in each template.

## 4. Weekly routine (about 2 hours a week)
- **Mon:** send 5 emails and make 5 calls from the list above
- **Wed:** follow up with anyone who hasn't replied after 5 days (one follow-up only)
- **Fri:** post one shake video to Instagram or TikTok and reply to new Google reviews
- Track who you contacted, their reply, and any booking in a simple spreadsheet
